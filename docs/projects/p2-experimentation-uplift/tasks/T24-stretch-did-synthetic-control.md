# T24 (stretch): Difference-in-differences or synthetic control

**Phase:** Stretch · **Estimate:** 4 h · **Depends on:** T01

## Ask

Estimate the effect of a public UK policy change with difference-in-differences or synthetic control.

## Why

Covers causal inference without randomisation, the natural complement to Parts A and B.

## How

- The brief doesn't name the policy or dataset. Choose one first and agree it before starting
  (public UK open data, clear licence, clear before/after date and comparison units).
- DiD with parallel-trends check, or synthetic control with placebo tests.
