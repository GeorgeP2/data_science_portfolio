# KIT suite profile: generator parameter ranges

Generated by `python -m fulfilment_optimisation.kit_profile` from 13,200 replications in 132 settings (32 OFAT, 100 LHS) of the KIT Manual Warehouse Order Picking benchmark (Barlang, Lehmann & Furmans, 2026, CC BY 4.0). Ranges are across settings.

**KIT orders are small**: 1-3 lines with one unit each (mean 1.25), against 5-25 items in the Henn & Wäscher instances. The generator takes its order-size distribution as a parameter so both regimes can be produced (T19).

| Module | Parameter | KIT range | Family | Evidence |

| `layout` | aisles | 4 to 40 (median 16) | uniform integer | the LHS design spans 5-40, independently of aisle length (r = -0.11) |
| `layout` | locations per aisle | 5 to 40 (median 17) | uniform integer | the LHS design spans 5-40 |
| `order_arrivals` | orders per shift (8 h) | 407 to 2000 (median 897) | Poisson around the expected count | variance / mean of the 100 replication counts per setting: 0.56 to 1.46 (median 1.03); 1 for Poisson, and ±0.28 is sampling noise at 100 replications |
